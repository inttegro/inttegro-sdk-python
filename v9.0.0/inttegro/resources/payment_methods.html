 14    Payment methods resource for securely storing and managing payment instruments.
 15
 16    Payment methods represent saved payment instruments (mobile money accounts, bank
 17    cards, etc.) that customers can reuse for faster checkout. Tokenization converts
 18    sensitive payment details into a secure token that you can safely store and charge
 19    repeatedly without handling raw payment credentials.

 23    2. Verify the payment method (triggers OTP for mobile money)
 24    3. Confirm verification with OTP
 25    4. Use the payment method ID for future charges
 26
 27    See https://studio.inttegro.com/charge-repeat-customers for detailed guides.

Payment methods resource for securely storing and managing payment instruments.

Payment methods represent saved payment instruments (mobile money accounts, bank cards, etc.) that customers can reuse for faster checkout. Tokenization converts sensitive payment details into a secure token that you can safely store and charge repeatedly without handling raw payment credentials.

The typical flow is:

  1. Tokenize payment details to create a payment method
  2. Verify the payment method (triggers OTP for mobile money)
  3. Confirm verification with OTP
  4. Use the payment method ID for future charges
